Ship Management & Technical Services

Reliable Ship Management Solutions

Ship management involves a range of services such as technical support, commercial, procurement, crewing, and safety management.

As part of our solutions, we provide complete ship management services to help keep vessels safe and in an optimum running condition. This helps your operations to experience minimum downtime.

We make this possible through a dedicated team of professionals both offshore and onboard. Our experienced team provides the above-mentioned services for most types of vessels. We are among the fastest growing ship managers in the region with 13 vessels of all types currently under management. These include bulkers, tankers, and container vessels.

Our network of offices in Singapore and India meets the highest standards and accreditations for quality, knowledge, and safety.

Technical Services

End-to-end technical care to keep your vessels safe, compliant, and performing at their peak.

Full technical management of vessels

Comprehensive oversight of machinery, maintenance, compliance, and documentation for every voyage.

Regular inspections and timely corrective action

Scheduled onboard and remote checks ensure issues are identified early and resolved before they impact operations.

Technical inspections for owners, banks, insurers & bareboat charterers

Independent condition reports that support asset valuation, financing decisions, and risk assessments for key stakeholders.

Ship specific, computerized planned maintenance system

Digital PMS tailored to each vessel, helping you track tasks, spares usage, and due dates in real time across the fleet.

Periodic analysis of fuel and lubricants

Lab-backed analysis that optimizes consumption, protects engines, and extends the life of critical components.

Monitoring of peak performance metrics

Continuous tracking of speed, consumption, and machinery parameters to maintain vessels in their optimal performance window.

Technical monitoring analysis & performance optimization

Data-driven analysis converts raw operating data into actionable insights to reduce off-hire, fuel burn, and breakdowns.

Superintendence for repairs, dry-docking & emergencies

Experienced superintendents coordinate shipyards, contractors, and class to keep projects on schedule and on budget.

Ensuring availability of correct spares on board

Smart inventory planning and timely procurement so critical parts are available when and where they are needed.
